Doug Turnbull

Doug Turnbull is a professor in the Department of Computer Science at Ithaca College. His primary research interests include music information retrieval, recommender systems, machine learning, and technology for social good, with a particular emphasis on helping people discover music from their local communities.

Doug's research focuses on developing novel applications that help people discover, enjoy, and benefit from music. During the summer of 2019, students in his JimiLab worked on three related music technology projects. Localify explored how a web application could help Spotify users discover local musicians. Long-Tail Playlist Recommendation focused on developing algorithms that introduce listeners to artists who are often overlooked by commercial music services. Content-Based Song Selection investigated the use of deep learning models to select the next song in a music playlist.

For more information, visit Doug's academic website and research blog or explore Doug's Lab. If you are interested in supporting local music communities, check out Localify.org. Students are welcome to stop by Doug's office to discuss their own ideas involving music technology, machine learning, or recommender systems.

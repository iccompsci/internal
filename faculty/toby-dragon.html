Toby Dragon

Prof. Dragon loves to tinker and has a wide variety of interests. His traditional research interests, supported by a significant publication record, include Computer Science Education (CSED), which focuses on helping people understand computers, and Technology Enhanced Learning (TEL), which explores how computers can help people learn.

In recent years, Prof. Dragon has been investigating how these educational interests intersect with emerging technologies in computer science, including game development, virtual environments, mixed reality, and physical computing, which bridges hardware and software through technologies such as robotics and 3D printing.

Over time, these technologies have become significantly more user-friendly, accessible, and affordable. The overarching goal of this research is to better understand how these increasingly powerful and accessible technologies can enhance our learning experiences.

This work raises some intriguing questions. Can these technologies spark students' curiosity and motivate them to learn more about the technology surrounding them? What skills do we need to develop to use these technologies effectively and build useful tools with them?

Come help answer these questions and more!

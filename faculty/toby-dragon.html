Toby Dragon

Prof. Dragon loves to tinker, and has a wide variety of interests. His traditional interests with significant publication record include:

  • Computer Science Education (CSED) — helping people understand computers
  • Technology Enhanced Learning (TEL) — using computers to help people learn

In the past years, Prof. Dragon has been investigating how his traditional educational interests overlap with specific technologies related to computer science, including:

  • Game Development, Virtual Environments, and Mixed Reality
  • Physical Computing bridging hardware and software, including robotics and 3D printing

All of these technologies have become significantly more user-friendly, accessible, and affordable over time. The overall focus of this new research is to better understand how these various advanced, easy-to-access technologies can be used to enhance our learning experiences.

Some questions we should ask ourselves:

  • Can these technologies be used to help intrigue and motivate students to learn more about the technology surrounding them?
  • What skills do we need to learn to successfully use and build useful tools with these technologies?

Come help answer these questions and more!

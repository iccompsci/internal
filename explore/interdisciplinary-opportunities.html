Why these combinations may work

Open a major below to explore how each highlighted computing minor could be applied in that field. These examples illustrate possible academic and professional connections; they do not represent formal course equivalencies or individualized degree planning advice.

Applied Physics4 highlighted connections

Data-Centric Computing: Data analysis and visualization help interpret laboratory measurements and large scientific datasets.

Computer Systems: Systems knowledge supports understanding how scientific instruments, computing hardware, and software work together.

App Development: Software development makes it possible to build simulation tools and interfaces for experiments.

Machine Learning: Machine learning can help identify patterns in experimental observations and computational models.

Education Studies1 highlighted connection

App Development: Creating interactive lessons, educational apps, and digital classroom tools connects software development with teaching practice.

Environmental Science1 highlighted connection

Data-Centric Computing: Data management, geographic datasets, and visualization support the study of ecosystems and environmental change.

Physics4 highlighted connections

Data-Centric Computing: Scientific datasets and numerical results benefit from structured analysis and visualization.

Computer Systems: Computing hardware, operating systems, and performance considerations matter in scientific instrumentation.

App Development: Students can create simulation software, experiment interfaces, and scientific tools.

Machine Learning: Data-driven modeling can support analysis of complex physical phenomena.

Physics-Engineering 3-24 highlighted connections

Data-Centric Computing: Organizing and interpreting engineering measurements complements quantitative design work.

Computer Systems: Understanding hardware–software interactions supports computer-based engineering systems.

App Development: Developing software tools supports simulations, prototyping, and engineering workflows.

Machine Learning: Predictive models can be applied to measurements and engineering systems.

Politics1 highlighted connection

Data-Centric Computing: Databases and analytic methods can support electoral, demographic, and public-policy research.

Psychology1 highlighted connection

App Development: Custom applications can present stimuli, collect responses, and support behavioral experiments.

Sociology1 highlighted connection

Data-Centric Computing: Structured data analysis supports work with surveys, population statistics, and social datasets.

Accounting1 highlighted connection

Data-Centric Computing: Managing, querying, and checking structured financial records complements accounting workflows.

Business Analytics2 highlighted connections

Data-Centric Computing: Database design, data preparation, and visualization underpin many business analytics tasks.

Machine Learning: Prediction and classification techniques can extend business analysis beyond descriptive reports.

Finance1 highlighted connection

Data-Centric Computing: Data-centric techniques help organize market data and examine financial trends.

Marketing2 highlighted connections

Data-Centric Computing: Working with customer and campaign data helps evaluate audiences and marketing outcomes.

App Development: Interactive web and mobile applications can deliver customer-facing digital experiences.

Sport Management1 highlighted connection

Data-Centric Computing: Event, audience, and organizational datasets can inform planning and performance analysis.

Creative Industries1 highlighted connection

App Development: Building interactive digital products and prototypes complements creative entrepreneurship and production.

Journalism2 highlighted connections

Data-Centric Computing: Working with public records and datasets supports data-driven reporting and investigation.

App Development: Interactive explainers and digital storytelling tools can make reporting more accessible.

Writing for FT&EM1 highlighted connection

Exercise Science1 highlighted connection

Public and Community Health1 highlighted connection

Resources

Selected, currently useful resources for course planning, careers, internships, undergraduate research, scholarships, and graduate study. Start with Ithaca College's advising and career services when making decisions about degree requirements or applications.

Advising and course planning

  • Computer Science catalog and course descriptions — Official information on current degree requirements, courses, and prerequisites.
  • Computer Science B.A. requirements — Current requirements for the B.A. in Computer Science.
  • Computer Science B.S. requirements — Current requirements for the B.S. in Computer Science.
  • Academic advising and student services — College guidance on advising and responsibility for degree planning.

Career planning and job searches

  • IC Center for Career Exploration and Development — Career advising, applications, interviews, networking, and appointments.
  • Finding a job or internship — IC guidance for searching, preparing applications, and using career services.
  • Handshake at IC — College-supported job and internship search platform for students and alumni.
  • AfterCollege — Entry-level opportunities and internships; a supplemental search option.
  • Career resources for women — IC's collection of career-development resources for women students and alumni.

Internships and undergraduate research

  • NSF Research Experiences for Undergraduates (REU) — Find research programs for undergraduates across NSF-supported fields; check eligibility and application details with each site.
  • Pathways to Science — Search undergraduate research placements, summer programs, and STEM internships.
  • ORISE undergraduate internships and fellowships — Research opportunities in national laboratories and federal facilities.
  • Internships.com — Additional internship listings; review any paid features before registering.
  • IC internship funding resources — College guidance on financial support for eligible internship experiences.

Accessibility and inclusive career resources

  • IC Student Accessibility Services — Information on access, accommodations, and related campus services.
  • IC neurodivergent and disability career resources — Career-development resources relevant to students and alumni with disabilities.
  • EARN job-seeker resources — Resources for job seekers with disabilities, including workplace and job-search guidance.

Scholarships and graduate study

  • NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program — Information on applying for NSF graduate research funding; consult current eligibility rules and deadlines.
  • ORISE scholarship opportunities — Scholarship and research-support opportunities through ORISE.
  • Peterson's graduate school search — Browse graduate programs and compare possible paths.
  • GradSchools.com — Explore graduate programs by field, degree level, and location.

Other IC academic support

  • Academic Support Center — Academic guidance, tutoring referrals, and connections to student-support offices.

External opportunities, eligibility requirements, application dates, and website addresses can change. Check the current information on each organization's site and consult an adviser when planning courses or graduate study.

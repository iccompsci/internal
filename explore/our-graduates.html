Our Graduates

Jon Burger ‘18

Since graduating from Ithaca College in 2018, Jon has built a career at Google, where he currently works as a Senior Software Engineer on display advertising projects in Boulder, Colorado. His journey into software engineering began with a digital marketing internship at Pulse Commerce, where he used his technical skills to help rebuild the company’s website. While at IC, Jon gained valuable experience working with shared code and version control while collaborating with Prof. Doug Turnbull and other students on the MegsRadio research project. He credits IC’s algorithms and software engineering courses with helping prepare him for technical interviews and collaborative development. Today, his work involves leading long-term projects, reviewing code, analyzing performance data, and collaborating with other engineers.

Vaibhav “VZ” Zaveri, ‘22

Vaibhav Zaveri, an Ithaca College alumnus, serves as a Cybersecurity Analyst at Consortium Networks, where he evaluates emerging security platforms, conducts security health checks, automates vulnerability tracking, and manages virtual testing environments across various ecosystems. After IC, Vaibhav earned his Master of Engineering in Cybersecurity from Duke University. During his graduate studies, he was awarded the 2023 (ISC)² Graduate Scholarship and ranked in the Top 11% nationally in the National Cyber League competition. Vaibhav credits his strong technical foundation to his time at IC, specifically his cybersecurity independent study with Professor John Barr and his work under Professor Ali Erkan implementing a municipal waste-routing algorithms project, where he applied the data structures concepts taught by Professor Toby Dragon. Offering advice to prospective tech professionals, Vaibhav emphasizes the importance of applied practice: "Try to implement whatever you learn."

Kerry Anne Buckman, ‘21

Two weeks after graduating from IC in 2021, Kerry Anne worked as a full time Software Engineer at Ursa Space Systems in Ithaca, NY. After 5 years in this position, she moved to Colorado and works remotely as a Technical Project Manager at the same company. While at IC, she fostered close relationships with peers and professors that opened her up to countless opportunities, including TA-ing for CS courses, attending the Grace Hopper Celebration on scholarship, residing as President of the Women in Computing club at IC, and heading the No Data Centers campaign in Ithaca. Kerry Anne’s advice for IC students is to not let AI inhibit their learning, and to be their own number one priority.

Jackson Gray '25

Since graduating from Ithaca College in 2026, Jackson Gray has worked as a Cyber Security Specialist at MC Dean, where he focuses on protecting his organization's systems and investigating security incidents. His responsibilities include investigating suspicious sign-ins and phishing attempts, identifying potential threats, and fixing software vulnerabilities. One of his projects involved developing scripts to update outdated Microsoft .NET components across company devices. Jackson credits his computer science background and programming experience at IC with giving him an advantage in cybersecurity, particularly when writing scripts and creating queries to detect potential threats. He also uses AI tools like Claude to assist with coding, emphasizing that understanding how code works is still essential. His advice to current CS students is to build professional connections, pursue cybersecurity certifications, and gain hands-on experience with industry tools.

Computer Science Minor

The Computer Science minor offers students outside the major a flexible way to study computing alongside another field. Students can choose courses that support their academic interests, whether those involve programming, quantitative work, or understanding the technology used in their discipline.

Why This Minor?

Unlike minors focused on a particular area of computing, the Computer Science minor allows students to shape their coursework around their interests and goals. It is intended for students who want a computing background without pursuing the full major.

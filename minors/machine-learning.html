Machine Learning Minor

Our Machine Learning minor gives students a focused introduction to artificial intelligence, data-driven problem-solving, and computational thinking. Designed to complement a wide range of majors, the minor helps students understand how AI systems work, how intelligent technologies are developed, and how they can be applied thoughtfully across disciplines. It prepares students to engage with emerging AI tools and ideas in ways that are both technically informed and socially responsible.

Why This Minor?

For a computer science major, the Machine Learning minor offers a focused route into machine learning and related computational techniques. It provides a recognizable area of technical specialization for students interested in developing and evaluating systems that learn from data.

For students outside computer science, the minor can add an analytical and computational perspective to their chosen discipline. It is particularly valuable when their field involves prediction, classification, pattern recognition, or decisions informed by large datasets.

One Possible Combination

A business major, for example, could pair their study of markets, organizations, or finance with machine learning to investigate demand forecasting, customer segmentation, or other data-informed business questions. The business degree provides the domain knowledge needed to interpret and question the results.

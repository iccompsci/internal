App Development Minor

The App Development minor focuses on designing, building, and testing software applications. Students develop programming skills they can use to create interactive tools for the web, mobile devices, and other computing environments. The minor can support interests ranging from practical software development to interactive media and games.

Why This Minor?

For computer science majors, the minor offers additional practice turning ideas into working software, with attention to implementation, testing, and the people who will use an application.

Students in other majors can use programming to create tools for their own fields. An application might help someone explore an idea, collect information, or interact with digital content.

Interdisciplinary Connections

Students in Applied Physics and Physics could build software for simulations, experiments, or scientific instruments. In the Physics-Engineering 3-2 program, application development can support prototypes and engineering workflows.

In Education Studies, students might develop interactive lessons or classroom tools. Psychology students could design an application that presents stimuli and records responses during an experiment.

Students in Marketing might build customer-facing websites or mobile applications. Creative Industries students could turn a product idea into an interactive prototype.

For Journalism, applications can take the form of interactive explainers or digital stories. Writing for FT&EM students could explore interactive narratives and multimedia experiences.

In the arts, Music Composition students might create composition tools or interactive instruments. Integrative Performing Arts Studies students could develop software for performances or multimedia installations.

App Development Minor

Our app-development minor provides the programming core of our curriculum. Students develop the skills needed to create applications for desktop, mobile, and web environments while learning principles that support effective, reliable, and user-centered software design. The minor also provides a foundation for future work in game development, allowing students to connect programming, design, and interactive technology in creative ways.

Why This Minor?

For a computer science major, the App Development minor offers a concentrated path into the design, implementation, testing, and delivery of software applications. It provides a practical focus for students interested in building products that people use directly.

For students in other majors, the minor can turn disciplinary ideas into usable digital tools. Combining a field of study with application development opens opportunities to create interactive experiences, resources, and services for that field's audience.

One Possible Combination

A student in journalism, television, digital media, or another media-related major could combine that work with App Development to create interactive stories, mobile publications, or audience-facing applications. Their media studies would shape the content and experience, while the minor would provide the technical means to build it.

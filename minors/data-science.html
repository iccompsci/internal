Data Science Minor

The Data Science minor is offered in collaboration with Ithaca College’s Mathematics department. For the current program overview and requirements, consult the Mathematics Department catalog page.

Why This Minor?

Data Science brings computing and mathematical methods together to study information and draw conclusions from it. The minor provides an interdisciplinary option for students interested in that approach; the Mathematics Department catalog is the source for its specific coursework and requirements.
